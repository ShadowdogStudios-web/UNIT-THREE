Be the Historian

WHO THREW THE BOMB?

The Haymarket bombing is one of history's great unsolved mysteries - and a perfect lesson in the limits of evidence. Eight men were convicted and four were hanged, but who actually threw the bomb has never been established.

What we know

What we know. A bomb was thrown; police and civilians died. The men convicted were anarchist labor leaders, several of whom were not even present when the bomb exploded, and none of whom was ever shown to have thrown it.

What we cannot know
